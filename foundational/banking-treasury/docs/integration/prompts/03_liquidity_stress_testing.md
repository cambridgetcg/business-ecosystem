# Research Prompt: Contingency Funding Plan Testing Methodology

## Research Objective

Develop a comprehensive methodology for testing Contingency Funding Plans (CFPs) without triggering actual stress or alarming stakeholders.

## Context

Research established that improvisation fails in crisis—pre-built playbooks are essential. The CFP must include credit facility draws, cash pool unwinding, asset sales, and board escalation protocols. But untested plans may have gaps only discovered during actual stress.

Key challenge: How do you validate that a CFP works without actually executing it?

## Research Questions

### Section 1: Regulatory Expectations

**Primary question:** What do regulators expect for CFP testing in non-bank corporates?

Investigate:
- Federal Reserve CFP guidance (primarily for banks, but principles apply)
- SEC disclosure requirements for liquidity risk management
- Rating agency expectations (Moody's, S&P) for liquidity management
- Industry best practices (AFP, NACHA, TMI publications)

**Desired output:**
- Regulatory requirement summary
- Testing frequency recommendations
- Documentation requirements

### Section 2: Testing Methodologies

**Primary question:** What are the available testing methods and their trade-offs?

Investigate:
- Tabletop exercise protocols
- Component testing approaches
- Shadow mode execution
- Full simulation exercises
- Post-event reviews

For each, document:
- What it tests
- What it doesn't test
- Risk level
- Resource requirements
- Stakeholder visibility

**Desired output:**
- Testing methodology comparison matrix
- Recommended testing calendar
- Sample tabletop exercise script

### Section 3: Bank Coordination

**Primary question:** How should corporates coordinate CFP testing with relationship banks?

Investigate:
- Bank relationship manager perspectives
- Credit facility documentation review frequency
- Draw notice verification processes
- Bank-side processing time validation

**Desired output:**
- Bank coordination protocol
- Questions to ask relationship managers
- Documentation checklist

### Section 4: Metrics and Success Criteria

**Primary question:** How do you measure CFP readiness?

Investigate:
- Timing benchmarks (how long should each step take?)
- Coverage metrics (what percentage of scenarios are covered?)
- Training metrics (who knows their role?)
- Documentation currency (when were procedures last updated?)

**Desired output:**
- CFP readiness scorecard
- KPI definitions
- Benchmark targets

### Section 5: Scenario Library

**Primary question:** What scenarios should be included in CFP testing?

Investigate:
- 2008 financial crisis lessons
- 2020 COVID liquidity crunch
- 2025 Treasury market stress
- Industry-specific scenarios
- Company-specific tail risks

**Desired output:**
- Scenario library template
- 10-15 sample scenarios with parameters
- Scenario severity classification

## Research Methodology

1. **Regulatory sources:** Fed, SEC, rating agencies
2. **Industry associations:** AFP (Association for Financial Professionals)
3. **Consulting firms:** Treasury management consultants' publications
4. **Case studies:** Public post-mortems from stress events

## Success Criteria

Research is complete when:
- [ ] Testing methodology is selected and justified
- [ ] Testing calendar is defined
- [ ] Scenario library is populated
- [ ] Success metrics are measurable
- [ ] Bank coordination protocol is documented
- [ ] Gap from questions/05 is addressed
