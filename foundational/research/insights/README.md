## Key Aha Moments

These insights fundamentally changed mental models during research integration:

1. **QBAI Elimination**: OBBBA 2025 removed the 10% QBAI reduction entirely from GILTI/NCTI
2. **Middleware as Single Point of Failure**: BaaS middleware creates systemic risk (Synapse proved this)
3. **Physical vs Notional Pooling**: Physical pooling creates actual IC loans; notional doesn't
4. **409A Penalties on Employees**: The 20% penalty tax falls on employees, not the company
5. **Day-Before-Sale Planning Eliminated**: OBBBA "at ANY TIME during year" rule ends timing arbitrage
6. **Economic Substance is Real**: BVI/Cayman enforce substance laws with strike-off penalties
7. **De-Risking as Business Reality**: Banks terminate relationships based on profitability
8. **Jurisdiction is Four-Dimensional**: Formation, qualification, nexus, and regulatory are INDEPENDENT

## Methodology

This research was conducted using the Purpose Prompter (PP) methodology:
1. **Territory Assessment**: Identified this as research/understanding work
2. **Deep Understanding**: Read and synthesized 35+ research documents
3. **Signal Detection**: Noticed "aha" moments and areas of curiosity
4. **Targeted Research**: Web searches for each curiosity area
5. **Documentation**: Individual findings files with sources
