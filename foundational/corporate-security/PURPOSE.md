# Corporate Security - Purpose & Mission

## Mission Statement

**To protect the organization's people, physical assets, information, and operational continuity from internal and external threats** — enabling the business ecosystem to operate safely, securely, and resiliently while maintaining the trust of employees, customers, and stakeholders.

---

## The Problem We Solve

### Business Challenge

Organizations face diverse security threats beyond IT/cyber:

1. **Physical vulnerability** - Facilities, equipment, and personnel exposed to unauthorized access, theft, or harm
2. **Personnel risk** - Inadequate vetting allowing bad actors into trusted positions
3. **Information exposure** - Sensitive business information mishandled or improperly classified
4. **Business disruption** - Lack of continuity planning leaving operations vulnerable to crises
5. **Fraud exposure** - Weak internal controls enabling internal fraud and asset misappropriation
6. **Vendor risk** - Third parties introducing security vulnerabilities into the ecosystem
7. **Incident chaos** - No coordinated response when security events occur

### What This Leads To

- **Asset loss** from theft, fraud, or destruction
- **Personnel harm** from inadequate physical security or crisis response
- **Information breaches** from improper handling of sensitive data
- **Operational paralysis** when crises occur without continuity plans
- **Reputational damage** from security failures or fraud
- **Legal liability** from negligent security practices

---

## Our Solution

### What Corporate Security Provides

A **comprehensive business protection platform** that:

1. **Manages physical security** - Facility access control, badge management, visitor tracking, surveillance
2. **Administers personnel security** - Background checks, clearances, periodic reinvestigation
3. **Governs information classification** - Data classification levels, handling procedures, marking requirements
4. **Ensures business continuity** - BCP development, crisis management, recovery planning, testing
5. **Prevents fraud** - Internal controls, segregation of duties, fraud detection, investigation
6. **Assesses vendor security** - Third-party risk evaluation, security questionnaires, ongoing monitoring
7. **Coordinates incident response** - Security incident management, breach notification, lessons learned
8. **Provides security governance** - Policies, risk assessments, metrics, and program oversight

### Out of Scope

**Not Corporate Security's Responsibility:**

- **Cybersecurity/IT security** - IT security team handles technical controls (we coordinate, not implement)
- **Legal investigations** - Complex legal matters escalate to legal counsel
- **HR disciplinary actions** - HR handles personnel actions based on our findings
- **Insurance claims** - Finance/legal handle claims (we provide incident documentation)
- **Regulatory compliance programs** - Compliance service manages regulatory adherence
- **Privacy program management** - Data privacy is a separate function

### Integration Points

Corporate Security **provides security context to**:
- **Compliance** - Control testing evidence, audit support
- **Entity Governance** - Personnel records for officers/directors
- **All services** - Security requirements and classifications

Corporate Security **consumes from**:
- **Entity Governance** - Personnel records, organizational structure
- **Compliance** - Regulatory security requirements
- **Banking-Treasury** - Fraud pattern data
- **KYC-Identity** - Identity verification for personnel

## Key Design Principles

### 1. Defense in Depth

Multiple layers of security controls ensure no single point of failure. Physical, procedural, and personnel controls work together.

### 2. Proportional Response

Security measures are proportionate to risk. High-value assets and sensitive information receive stronger protection.

### 3. Need-to-Know Access

Information and facility access granted only to those with legitimate business need, at the minimum level required.

### 4. Resilience Over Prevention

While prevention is primary, the system assumes some incidents will occur and emphasizes rapid detection, response, and recovery.

### 5. Security as Enabler

Security exists to enable business operations, not obstruct them. Controls are designed to be effective without being burdensome.

### Business Metrics
- **Zero security breaches** resulting in material asset loss
- **Business continuity**: <4 hour recovery for critical functions during disruption
- **Fraud prevention**: Fraud losses below industry benchmark
- **Vendor security**: No security incidents originating from third parties

## Non-Goals

**What We Explicitly Do Not Aim To Do:**

1. **Replace IT security** - We handle business/physical security, not cybersecurity
2. **Conduct all investigations** - Complex legal matters go to legal counsel
3. **Manage all employee training** - Security awareness only, not general training
4. **Handle insurance claims** - We document incidents, finance handles claims
5. **Provide personal security** - Executive protection is specialized and separate

## Conclusion

Corporate Security serves as the **protective shield of the business ecosystem**. By managing physical security, personnel vetting, information protection, and business continuity, it enables the organization to operate confidently in an uncertain world.

**Our success is measured by the absence of security incidents, the speed of crisis recovery, and the trust employees and stakeholders place in our protective measures.**
