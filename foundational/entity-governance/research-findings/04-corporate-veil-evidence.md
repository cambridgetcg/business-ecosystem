# Research Finding: The Corporate Veil Is Evidence-Based

## The Aha Moment

**Before Integration**: Corporate veil = legal status you either have or don't have

**After Integration**: Corporate veil = accumulated EVIDENCE of separation. Every formality is a brick in the wall; every omission is ammunition for plaintiffs.

## What Courts Actually Look At

### The Alter Ego Factors

When plaintiffs sue to pierce the veil, courts examine evidence in multiple categories:

```
FACTOR CATEGORY 1: FORMALITY EVIDENCE
─────────────────────────────────────
□ Are annual meetings held?
□ Are board meetings held for major decisions?
□ Are minutes kept for all meetings?
□ Are resolutions documented for significant actions?
□ Are stock records properly maintained?
□ Are amendments properly filed?

Evidence Weight: Each missing item = evidence AGAINST separation
```

```
FACTOR CATEGORY 2: FINANCIAL SEPARATION
───────────────────────────────────────
□ Separate bank accounts?
□ Separate accounting records?
□ Entity pays its own expenses?
□ Intercompany loans properly documented?
□ Arm's length transactions?
□ Adequate capitalization?

Evidence Weight: Commingling = STRONGEST evidence for piercing
```

```
FACTOR CATEGORY 3: OPERATIONAL INDEPENDENCE
───────────────────────────────────────────
□ Own employees?
□ Own office space?
□ Own phone/email?
□ Signs contracts in own name?
□ Own business licenses?
□ Own insurance policies?

Evidence Weight: Shared operations = evidence of common enterprise
```

```
FACTOR CATEGORY 4: IDENTITY DISTINCTION
───────────────────────────────────────
□ Different officers than parent?
□ Different directors than parent?
□ Uses own letterhead?
□ Represents itself to third parties as separate?
□ Third parties understand it's separate?

Evidence Weight: Confusion about identity = evidence against separation
```

### The Balancing Act

Courts don't require ALL factors. They BALANCE:

| Scenario | Likely Outcome |
|----------|----------------|
| All formalities + adequate capital + separation | Veil upheld (strong evidence) |
| Most formalities + some commingling | May survive (depends on severity) |
| Few formalities + adequate capital | Risky (formalities matter) |
| Many formalities + undercapitalized | Very risky (capital trumps formalities) |
| No formalities + commingled + undercapitalized | Pierced (no evidence of separation) |

### Transaction-Triggered Requirements

```
WHEN THESE EVENTS HAPPEN → DOCUMENT THIS
────────────────────────────────────────

Event: Hiring an officer
Document: Board resolution appointing officer
         Employment agreement (if applicable)
         Officer accepting appointment

Event: Opening a bank account
Document: Board resolution authorizing account
         List of authorized signers
         Banking resolutions (bank's form)

Event: Major contract (material to business)
Document: Board resolution approving contract
         Terms reviewed and approved
         Signing authority confirmed

Event: Loan (to or from entity)
Document: Board resolution authorizing loan
         Promissory note with arm's length terms
         Repayment schedule
         If intercompany: formal loan agreement

Event: Distribution/Dividend
Document: Board resolution declaring distribution
         Determination that surplus exists
         Record date and payment date
         For LLC: capital account adjustment

Event: Issuance of equity
Document: Board resolution authorizing issuance
         Shareholder approval (if required)
         Stock certificate or unit certificate
         Updated cap table/stock ledger
```

## Parent-Subsidiary Separation Evidence

### The Special Case

Parent-subsidiary liability is the most common piercing scenario:

```
PARENT-SUBSIDIARY EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS
───────────────────────────────────────

Parent Corp                        Subsidiary Corp
     │                                   │
     │                                   │
     └───────────── MUST BE ─────────────┘
                   GENUINELY
                   SEPARATE

The wall between them needs EVIDENCE:
```

### Separation Checklist

```
OPERATIONAL SEPARATION
──────────────────────
□ Subsidiary has own employees (or properly contracted from parent)
□ Subsidiary has own office (or pays rent to parent at market rate)
□ Subsidiary has own phone/email systems
□ Subsidiary has own website (if customer-facing)
□ Subsidiary makes own business decisions

FINANCIAL SEPARATION
────────────────────
□ Separate bank accounts (CRITICAL)
□ Subsidiary pays its own bills
□ No commingling of funds
□ Intercompany loans properly documented with interest
□ Intercompany services at arm's length pricing
□ Transfer pricing documented
□ Subsidiary adequately capitalized at formation

GOVERNANCE SEPARATION
─────────────────────
□ Subsidiary has own board (some parent overlap OK, not 100%)
□ Subsidiary board meets independently
□ Subsidiary board makes real decisions
□ Subsidiary has independent officers where possible
□ Subsidiary maintains own books and records
□ Subsidiary has own registered agent

CONTRACT SEPARATION
───────────────────
□ Subsidiary signs contracts in own name
□ Parent doesn't guarantee subsidiary contracts (or documented if does)
□ Third parties understand they're dealing with subsidiary
□ Invoices and payments in subsidiary's name
□ Separate business licenses/permits
```

## Veil Health Scoring

### Concept

Entity governance systems should provide a "veil health score" based on evidence accumulation:

```
VEIL HEALTH SCORE COMPONENTS
────────────────────────────

FORMALITY SCORE (0-100)
├── Annual meeting held? (+20)
├── Board meets quarterly? (+15)
├── All major decisions documented? (+25)
├── Proper minutes maintained? (+20)
└── Stock records current? (+20)

FINANCIAL SCORE (0-100)
├── Separate bank account? (+30)
├── No commingling evidence? (+30)
├── Adequate capitalization? (+20)
└── Intercompany loans documented? (+20)

SEPARATION SCORE (0-100) [for subsidiaries]
├── Distinct officers? (+20)
├── Independent operations? (+20)
├── Contracts in own name? (+20)
├── Third parties recognize separation? (+20)
└── Intercompany agreements current? (+20)

OVERALL VEIL HEALTH = Weighted average
├── Single entity: (Formality × 0.5) + (Financial × 0.5)
└── Subsidiary: (Formality × 0.3) + (Financial × 0.4) + (Separation × 0.3)
```

### Thresholds

| Score | Status | Risk Level |
|-------|--------|------------|
| 90-100 | Excellent | Low piercing risk |
| 70-89 | Good | Moderate risk |
| 50-69 | Warning | Elevated risk |
| < 50 | Critical | High piercing risk |

### Alert Triggers

```
VEIL HEALTH ALERTS
──────────────────

CRITICAL (immediate attention):
○ No annual meeting in 18+ months
○ Bank account commingling detected
○ Major contract signed without resolution
○ Intercompany transfer without documentation

WARNING (attention within 30 days):
○ No board meeting in 9+ months
○ Officer change without documentation
○ Intercompany loan missing interest payment
○ Missing minutes for documented decision

INFORMATIONAL (track):
○ Annual meeting due in 60 days
○ Board meeting not held in 6 months
○ Intercompany agreement up for renewal
```

## Practical Application

### Pre-Litigation Preparation

If litigation is anticipated, the veil defense requires:

1. **Gather all formality evidence**
   - Complete meeting minutes for entire corporate history
   - All resolutions (especially for disputed transactions)
   - Stock ledger and ownership records

2. **Demonstrate financial separation**
   - Bank statements showing separate accounts
   - Accounting records showing proper allocation
   - Intercompany loan documentation with payments

3. **Prove operational independence**
   - Contracts signed in entity's name
   - Employee records showing entity as employer
   - Business licenses in entity's name

4. **Show governance independence**
   - Board meeting minutes showing deliberation
   - Evidence of independent business judgment
   - Officer decisions made at entity level

### The Entity Governance System Role

The system should:
- **Generate** required formality documentation
- **Track** formality compliance over time
- **Alert** when formalities are overdue
- **Score** veil health for early warning
- **Produce** evidence packages for litigation

## Summary

The corporate veil is not a binary legal status - it's a spectrum of protection based on accumulated evidence. Entity governance systems should:

1. **Track formality compliance** proactively
2. **Generate required documentation** automatically where possible
3. **Monitor separation evidence** for parent-subsidiary relationships
4. **Calculate veil health scores** for early warning
5. **Produce evidence packages** for potential litigation

**The Takeaway**: Every documented meeting is a brick in the wall. Every skipped formality is a hole the plaintiffs will exploit.
