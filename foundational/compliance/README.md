# Compliance

Regulatory compliance, internal controls, and audit management.

## Subdomains

### compliance-program
Core compliance framework and policies.
- `compliance-policy.ts` - Policy documents
- `compliance-procedure.ts` - Implementing procedures
- `compliance-calendar.ts` - Recurring compliance activities
- `policy-version.ts` - Policy version control
- `policy-acknowledgment.ts` - Employee attestations

### regulatory-mapping
Applicable regulations and requirements.
- `regulation.ts` - Regulatory frameworks (SOX, GDPR, etc.)
- `regulatory-requirement.ts` - Specific requirements
- `jurisdiction-applicability.ts` - Where regulations apply
- `regulatory-change.ts` - New/amended regulations
- `control-mapping.ts` - Requirements to controls mapping

### compliance-training
Employee compliance education.
- `training-course.ts` - Course catalog
- `training-assignment.ts` - Employee assignments
- `training-completion.ts` - Completion records
- `certification.ts` - Compliance certifications
- `training-schedule.ts` - Training calendars

### internal-audit
Internal audit program management.
- `audit-plan.ts` - Annual audit plan
- `audit-engagement.ts` - Individual audits
- `control-test.ts` - Control testing procedures
- `audit-finding.ts` - Identified issues
- `audit-workpaper.ts` - Supporting documentation

### external-audit
Regulatory examinations and third-party audits.
- `regulatory-examination.ts` - Regulator examinations
- `audit-request.ts` - Information requests
- `pbc-list.ts` - Prepared By Client document lists
- `management-letter.ts` - Auditor communications
- `audit-opinion.ts` - Audit opinions and reports

### remediation
Finding remediation and corrective actions.
- `remediation-item.ts` - Items requiring remediation
- `corrective-action.ts` - Planned corrective actions
- `action-plan.ts` - Remediation project plans
- `remediation-status.ts` - Progress tracking
- `root-cause.ts` - Root cause analysis

### compliance-reporting
Compliance metrics and board reporting.
- `compliance-report.ts` - Periodic compliance reports
- `board-report.ts` - Board/committee reports
- `regulatory-filing.ts` - Required regulatory filings
- `compliance-metric.ts` - Compliance KPIs
- `kri.ts` - Key Risk Indicators

### whistleblower
Ethics hotline and investigation management.
- `whistleblower-report.ts` - Anonymous reports
- `anonymous-channel.ts` - Reporting channels
- `investigation.ts` - Investigation records
- `retaliation-claim.ts` - Retaliation allegations
- `resolution.ts` - Case resolutions

## Key Integrations

- **All apps**: Compliance requirements apply across the ecosystem
- **entity-governance**: Entity-level compliance
- **kyc-identity**: AML compliance and reporting
- **data-privacy**: Privacy regulation compliance
- **accounting**: SOX and financial controls
